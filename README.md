# Zepto-Analysis
An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze Zepto’s sales and inventory performance for the year 2025.
📊 Zepto Sales & Inventory Analysis | Power BI Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze Zepto’s sales and inventory performance for the year 2025.

The objective is to derive meaningful business insights related to:

Sales trends

Category performance

Pricing strategy

Inventory health

Customer demand patterns

This analysis supports decision-making for quick-commerce operations, where inventory availability and demand forecasting are critical.

Analyze monthly and daily sales trends

🔍 Key Insights
🔹 Sales Performance

Total revenue generated: ₹49.03B

Monthly sales range between ₹3.8B – ₹4.2B, indicating consistent demand.

🔹 Category Analysis

Top Revenue Contributors:

Chocolates & Candies – ₹6.9B

Ice Cream & Desserts – ₹6.9B

Packaged Food – ₹6.9B

Lower Performing Categories:

Beverages

Home & Cleaning

Meats, Fish & Eggs

These categories may require pricing or promotional optimization.

🔹 Price Category Insights

Budget segment has the highest product count.

Mid-range products generate strong and stable revenue.

Premium products contribute the least to total sales.

🔹 Inventory Insights

Healthy Stock: 99.04% of total sales

Low Stock: 0.72%

Out of Stock: < 1%

This indicates efficient inventory management but also highlights the need for monitoring fast-moving items.

🔹 Day-Wise Trend

Wednesday recorded the highest sales (~₹7.1B).

Sales remain consistent across all weekdays, showing steady customer demand.
